Who We Are

Nurturing Futures, Building Hope

Rang'ombe CDC is a ministry of the Rang'ombe Full Gospel Church committed to nurturing children and youth through education, mentorship, healthcare, spiritual growth, and skills development.

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to thrive. By partnering with Compassion Kenya, we provide a safe, supportive environment where children can discover their potential and break the cycle of poverty.
